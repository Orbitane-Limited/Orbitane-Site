# Orbitane — Site Brief

> The founding document for orbitane.com's design and build. Written to be read by the team, by Claude, and by clients.

## Who Orbitane is

A product house building **AI/ML products and native macOS software**. First shipped product: **Hemta**, a native Mac download manager. More products will follow — the site must scale to hold them without redesign.

## What the site must achieve

The site is Orbitane's first product demo. A visitor should stop mid-scroll and think: *"whoever built this page thinks differently — imagine what their products are like."* Every animation must read as **proof of craft, not decoration**. Cinematic, but lightweight; imaginative, but engineered.

## Company values the site must carry

- **We own customer requirements.** Ownership, not order-taking: clients hand us a problem and it becomes ours until it's solved.
- **We care about the people behind the requirements.** The site's warmth and clarity should reflect that; no cold agency posturing.
- Craft as identity: the products (and this site) are the argument.

## The concept: THE ORBITAL SYSTEM ✅ approved

The site's structure is drawn from the name. The visitor's scroll is a **trajectory through the Orbitane system**:

1. Open in quiet, dark space — the Orbitane core comes into focus.
2. Each product is a **body in orbit** — the journey passes each one, and each gets its own cinematic moment (Hemta and its knot first).
3. Future products appear as faint, unlit orbits — "more in motion."
4. Values/about/contact are the return trajectory to the core.

Every new product = a new orbit. The structure itself demonstrates imagination and scales forever.

## Reference alignment (chosen by the founder)

| Reference | What we take from it |
|---|---|
| **family.co** | The feel: buttery, springy, weightless simplicity; motion that carries WITH the user; zero bloat |
| **igloo.inc** | The structure: a 3D scroll journey with a clear mental map; the "stop and stare" reaction |
| **superlist.com** | The personality: playful product motion; software with a smile |

Execution lane: **engineered motion** — GSAP + smooth scroll (Lenis-class), one tasteful canvas/3D scene for the orbital core if needed; NOT a heavy WebGL site. Performance and reduced-motion discipline are non-negotiable (same gates as the Hemta build).

## Client-facing requirement

The planning artifacts of this project — **mindmap, sitemap, design system, motion spec** — will be shown to Orbitane's clients as proof of process. They must be written and diagrammed at presentation quality, not as internal scratch notes.

## Working agreements (inherited, non-negotiable)

- All git/GitHub operations are performed by the founder personally. Claude never commits, pushes, pulls, or uses gh (enforced in `.claude/settings.json`).
- Honest content only. No invented clients, testimonials, or metrics.
- Design decisions get documented in `docs/` before build; docs are the contract.
